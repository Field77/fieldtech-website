We are building a nationwide commercial sales network and are currently recruiting self-employed brokers who want flexibility, strong commission potential and access to multiple business product categories.

CURRENT OPPORTUNITY

BROKER — SELF EMPLOYED

IRELAND-WIDESELF-EMPLOYEDCOMMISSION BASEDFLEXIBLE

As a FieldTech Broker, you will represent selected commercial products and services to businesses and customers across Ireland. You will identify opportunities, understand customer requirements, present suitable solutions and manage the sales process professionally from initial conversation through to submission.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Develop new business opportunities in your chosen area or network.
  • Speak with business owners and decision-makers.
  • Understand customer requirements and current commercial setup.
  • Present suitable FieldTech products and partner solutions.
  • Collect accurate information and submit completed opportunities.
  • Maintain professional, honest and transparent customer communication.
  • Follow product, compliance and campaign procedures.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

  • Strong communication and people skills.
  • Commercial awareness and confidence speaking with businesses.
  • Self-motivation and the ability to manage your own activity.
  • Professional conduct and attention to detail.
  • Sales experience is useful but not essential where the right attitude is present.
  • Ability to learn multiple product categories.
  • Comfort working independently while following FieldTech processes.

WHAT FIELDTECH PROVIDES

  • Product and sales training.
  • Access to Energy, Merchant Services, Telecoms and Business Finance propositions.
  • Campaign information, commercial support and sales materials.
  • Ongoing account and operational support.
  • Flexible working structure.
  • Performance-based earning with no artificial earnings cap.

IMPORTANT

  • This is a self-employed contractor opportunity, not a salaried employee role.
  • Earnings depend on completed sales and applicable commission terms.
  • You are expected to represent FieldTech and its commercial partners professionally.
  • All customer interactions must follow the relevant campaign and compliance requirements.
